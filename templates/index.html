How it works

      

        
1 · Key derivation
Your password and a random 16-byte salt go through PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 rounds) to make a 256-bit key.

        
2 · AES-256 encryption
The message is sealed with AES-256-GCM, so a wrong password or any tampering is detected.

        
3 · LSB hiding
Ciphertext bits replace the least significant bit of each colour value. The change is invisible to the eye.
